How I Work

From messy fragmented data to a live boardroom dashboard in weeks, not months. A seamless, transparent workflow designed for business owners.

1
Discovery & Audit

We start with a deep dive into your business goals. I audit your current spreadsheets, databases, and APIs to identify exactly where your data lives and what KPIs matter most for your growth.

2
Data Engineering

I clean, transform, and model your messy data. Whether writing Python scripts or SQL procedures, I build an automated, error-free pipeline that acts as your single source of truth.

3
Dashboard Architecture

I design premium, highly intuitive Power BI or Tableau dashboards. Complex DAX measures are written to ensure every metric is filterable, accurate, and visually stunning.

4
Deployment & Training

Your dashboards go live. I set up automated daily refreshes and role-level security. Finally, I provide recorded training so your team knows exactly how to extract insights instantly.
